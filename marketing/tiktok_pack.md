## Setup (once)

**Record on your phone, not your PC.** Open the template in the Google Sheets
app and screen-record. Three reasons: it's vertical natively (no cropping),
your finger tapping reads as "real person using it", and it proves the
"works on your phone" claim without saying it.

- **iPhone:** Control Centre → Screen Record
- **Android:** swipe down → Screen recorder
- **PC fallback:** `Win + G` (Xbox Game Bar) → record → crop to 9:16 in CapCut

**Profile bio:**

Link: `nasritools.etsy.com`

**Editing:** CapCut (free). Add the on-screen text, one trending quiet sound,
export 1080×1920.

## The rule that decides everything

**The first 1.5 seconds.** Text must be on screen before the viewer's thumb
moves, and something must already be happening. No intros, no logos, no
"hey guys". Start mid-action.

*Your strongest one. You actually trade — that's an edge nobody can copy.*

**Length:** 30s

## What kills these videos

- A logo or intro card at the start — dead in 1 second
- Text that appears after the visual instead of with it
- Recording a desktop window and letterboxing it (black bars = scroll)
- Saying "link in bio" more than once
- Music louder than the moment — pick something quiet and steady

## What to watch

After 5 videos, check which one held attention longest (TikTok Analytics →
average watch time, not views). Make two more like that one. Ignore likes —
watch time is the only number that moves the algorithm.
