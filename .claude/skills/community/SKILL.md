# community

Read `product-marketing` first.

## Our three community surfaces

**1. Public Telegram channel** — delayed signals, education, weekly recaps.
The proof archive and the top of the funnel (`paywalls`). Broadcast-only;
comments invite spam and moderation load we cannot staff.

**2. Private subscriber channel** — live alerts. Signal-to-noise here is the
product. Rules: no promotion ever (ours included, per `sms`), no off-topic,
no other members' trade calls. If discussion is wanted, give it a separate
chat group so the alert channel stays clean.

**3. Other people's communities** — Discords, forums, FinTwit. We are guests.
See `social`: contribute, never promote, let people ask.

## Rhythm for the public channel

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Pre-session | The levels being watched — PDH/PDL, Asia H/L, with the chart |
| Post-session | What the system flagged, with outcomes, losses included |
| Weekly | The recap (`emails`, `video`) |
| Ad hoc | One mechanism explainer per week (`content`) |

Consistency matters more than volume. A channel that posts every session
builds the habit; a channel that posts in bursts trains people to mute it.

## Moderation

- Ban immediately: other signal services promoting, "DM me" account-management
  scams, anyone asking members for money. This niche is a target; letting one
  through costs the trust of everyone who saw it.
- No price debates in public — answer once with the `pricing` facts and move on.
- Do not argue with a losing-week critic. Post the loss data and let it stand.

## The engagement that actually matters

Replies, not reactions. A subscriber who asks "why did it skip the 10:15
sweep?" is engaged and retained; a subscriber who never speaks is churning
(`churn-prevention`). Answer every question, publicly and technically. The
answers double as `content`.

## Growth

Communities grow by being forwardable. Every post should stand alone — a
marked chart with the instrument, time and level visible is forwardable; a
post referencing "the setup from earlier" is not.
