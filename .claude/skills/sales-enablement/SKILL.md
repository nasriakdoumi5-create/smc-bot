# sales-enablement

Read `product-marketing` first.

## Where selling actually happens

Subscriptions are self-serve (`signup-flow`). Real conversations happen for:
- Bot-as-a-service builds (highest ticket).
- Partner and bundle deals (`co-marketing`).
- Educators buying a feed for their members.

## The objection set

Prepare these; they recur almost verbatim.

**"How do I know this isn't another scam?"**
The only honest answer is evidence, delivered without defensiveness: the
public delayed-signal archive including losses, the backtest range on named
data, the fact that we publish stopped-out trades. Never counter with
rhetoric — offer the archive link and stop talking.

**"What's the win rate?"**
Give the real distribution from a named backtest with symbol, timeframe and
date range, labeled hypothetical (`product-marketing`), and say plainly that
live results differ. A precise, caveated answer builds more trust than a
confident number, and a confident number is a claim-rule violation.

**"Why only two sessions?"**
This is the strongest part of the product. Outside kill zones the setups
degrade; the system refusing to fire is the feature. Lead with it.

**"Can I see it live first?"**
No trial (`paywalls`), but yes to: the public channel, the weekly recap, and
a screen-share walkthrough. Offer the walkthrough — it closes technical
buyers.

**"What if I can't take every signal?"**
Nobody takes every signal. Star ratings exist for exactly this; explain the
1–3 point confluence scale and let them filter.

**"It's expensive."**
Reframe against their actual cost basis: one avoided drawdown breach on a
funded account. Do not discount (`pricing`).

## The demo

10 minutes, in this order: live chart with a marked historical setup →
the alert JSON → the same alert rendered in Telegram → the outcome →
one loss, shown deliberately → pricing. Showing a loss inside the demo is the
single highest-converting move available in this category.

## Follow-up kit

One email after every conversation: what was discussed, the archive link, the
price, and the next step. No attachment decks — a link to a real page
(`site-architecture`) beats a PDF nobody opens.

## Never

Push a bad fit (crypto-only traders, beginners, anyone wanting managed
money). They churn, they refund, and they post about it. Disqualifying
quickly is a sales skill, not a failure.
