# co-marketing

Read `product-marketing` first.

## Why partnerships fit this business

Our ICP is concentrated in places we do not own: prop-firm communities,
educator Discords, futures newsletters. Paid access to them is restricted
(`ads`); partnership access is not. One newsletter partnership can deliver
more qualified subscribers than a quarter of content.

## Partner types, ranked by fit

1. **Futures educators** with paid communities. They need signal
   infrastructure; we need their audience. Strongest fit.
2. **Prop-firm adjacent tools** — journaling apps, risk calculators,
   analytics dashboards. Shared audience, no product overlap.
3. **Newsletter owners** in the futures/day-trading niche — see `ads` on
   sponsorships, which often begin as partnerships.
4. **Pine developers** with complementary indicators. Bundle possibilities.

Avoid: anyone selling signals (direct competitor), anyone with account
management or copy-trading offerings (regulatory exposure by association),
and anyone whose marketing violates our claim rules — association transfers.

## Formats that work

- **Joint session recap** — their commentary, our flagged setups, one video
  (`video`), published to both audiences.
- **Tool integration** — our webhook into their journaling product. Real
  utility, durable link, and it showcases the plumbing.
- **Guest teaching** — a mechanism explainer written for their newsletter
  (`content` rules apply), no pitch, one attribution line.
- **Bundle** — their course + our channel at a combined price. Requires
  `pricing` sign-off on the margin and `revops` capacity for the intake.

## Making the ask

Via `cold-email`. Lead with what they get, and be specific about volume — do
not imply an audience we do not have. Overstating our size is discovered
immediately in this niche and ends the relationship.

Propose the smallest viable collaboration first: one joint post, not an
annual partnership agreement.

## Before agreeing

- [ ] Their marketing claims reviewed against `product-marketing` rules.
- [ ] Fulfillment capacity confirmed (`revops`) — a partnership that lands 50
      signups in a day breaks manual provisioning.
- [ ] Attribution agreed (unique UTM or a code, per `analytics`).
- [ ] Revenue share terms written down, if any (`referrals`).
- [ ] An exit: how either side ends it without drama.
