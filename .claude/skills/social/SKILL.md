# social

Read `product-marketing` first — including why paid is mostly closed to us,
which makes this skill load-bearing rather than supplementary.

## Where the ICP actually is

| Surface | Role | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| X/Twitter | Primary discovery. FinTwit futures niche is small and reachable. | Daily during sessions |
| Telegram public channel | Proof archive + conversion path (`paywalls`) | Every session |
| TradingView ideas | Discovery *and* `aso` ranking support | 2–3/week |
| YouTube | Long-form trust, feeds `video` | Weekly |
| Reddit / Discord | High risk, high value — see rules | Participate, do not post |

## The post types that work

1. **The marked chart.** One clean screenshot, the sweep marked, one line of
   text with the time and level. No commentary needed. Highest reach per
   effort we have.
2. **The called-it-before.** Post the level *before* the session, then the
   outcome after. Timestamped prediction is the only credibility currency
   FinTwit accepts. Post them whether they work or not.
3. **The loss post.** A stopped-out trade, explained. Reliably outperforms
   winners on engagement and dramatically outperforms on conversion.
4. **The mechanism thread.** 5–7 posts teaching one concept, chart per post,
   ending with the `/learn/` article, not the product.
5. **The build log.** Webhook payloads, Pine snippets. Attracts the technical
   segment and the bot-as-a-service buyer.

## Rules

- Never post a winner without having posted a loser that week.
- Timestamps and instrument on every chart. Unlabeled charts read as stolen.
- No profit screenshots, no account balances, no "clients made $X"
  (`product-marketing`).
- Reply more than you post. The niche is small; recognition compounds faster
  through replies than through broadcasts.
- Watermark chart images (`image`) — they will be reposted.
- Arabic and English posts on separate channels or clearly separated, not
  mixed in one post.

## Reddit and Discord specifically

Self-promotion gets banned, and a ban in r/FuturesTrading or a large Discord
is permanent and costly. Rule: contribute for weeks, never link the product,
let people ask. If a link is ever posted it should be to a free tool
(`free-tools`), never to `/pricing`.
