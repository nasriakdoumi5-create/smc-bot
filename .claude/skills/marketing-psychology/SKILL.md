# marketing-psychology

Read `product-marketing` first.

## The governing insight

This audience has been marketed to by scammers, repeatedly, using the entire
standard persuasion toolkit. That has inverted several classic tactics: the
signals that normally increase conversion now mark the sender as fraudulent.
Persuasion here works through **costly signals of honesty**, not through
pressure.

## Tactics that backfire with traders

| Classic tactic | Effect here | Use instead |
|---|---|---|
| Urgency / countdown | Scam marker | A stated, unchanging price |
| Scarcity ("3 spots left") | Scam marker | Honest capacity limits (`revops`) |
| Social proof by volume ("10,000 members") | Suspicion of bought numbers | Engagement quality, visible replies |
| Profit screenshots | Immediate disqualification | Loss posts and unedited history |
| Authority signaling (luxury imagery) | Contempt | Domain-correct vocabulary |
| Reciprocity via gated freebies | Mild irritation | Ungated tools (`free-tools`) |

## What works instead

**Costly honesty.** Publishing losses is expensive for a fraudster to imitate
because it contradicts their claims. That is precisely why it converts: it is
a signal only an honest operator can afford to send. It is the most
underpriced marketing asset available to us.

**Precision as proof.** "3-point confluence, 07:00–11:00 UTC, MNQ 5m" carries
authority that adjectives cannot, because specificity is checkable and
fraudsters avoid checkable claims.

**Loss aversion, aimed correctly.** The ICP's live fear is breaching a funded
account's drawdown rule — not missing a winner. Frame around rule enforcement
and avoided damage, never around missed profits. Every "don't miss out" frame
is aimed at the wrong fear.

**Refusal as a signal.** A system that declines to fire outside kill zones
signals discipline. Products that say no are trusted more than products that
say yes to everything.

**Consistency and commitment.** Daily pre-session level posts build a habit;
the habit, not the pitch, produces the subscription (`community`).

**Anchoring, used honestly.** The $30 indicator bundle makes $45 for
everything read as obviously correct (`pricing`). This is legitimate
structure, not manipulation — every price is real and purchasable.

## The ethical line

We use these to make a real product legible, never to make a weak one
attractive. Concretely: no manufactured scarcity, no invented deadlines, no
claim we cannot substantiate, and no framing that implies certainty in a
probabilistic system. If a technique only works when the buyer is
misinformed, it is off-limits.
