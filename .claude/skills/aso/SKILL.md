# aso

Read `product-marketing` first.

## The real "app store" here

We do not ship a mobile app. Our equivalent stores are:

1. **TradingView script library** — the primary discovery surface. An
   invite-only script still appears in search and on the profile.
2. **Telegram channel directories** and in-app search.
3. Broker/tool marketplaces where relevant (see `directory`).

## TradingView listing rules — read before writing

House rules get scripts removed, and removal loses the ranking history:

- No external links, prices, contact details, or promotional language in the
  script description.
- No performance promises or claimed returns.
- Describe what the script *does* and how to use it. Sell in the profile bio
  and signature, which have different rules.
- Invite-only scripts must still have a complete, honest description.

## Anatomy of a listing that converts

**Title** — carries the searchable terms. `Kill Zone Sweep Pro — Liquidity
Sweep + Reversal` works: the feature words are the search words.

**First two lines** — this is all most users read before deciding. State the
mechanism and the instrument:
> Identifies institutional liquidity sweeps above PDH/PDL, Asia session H/L
> and swing highs/lows, during London and New York kill zones.

**Feature block** — scannable, specific, numeric:
- PDH/PDL sweep: 3 points (highest priority)
- Asia H/L sweep: 2 points
- Swing H/L sweep: 1 point
- Fires only 07:00–11:00 and 13:30–17:00 UTC
- Quality rating 1–3 stars by confluence

**Fit statement** — `Best on: MNQ, NQ — 5 minute chart`. Stating what it is
*not* for reduces bad-fit installs, which protects the rating.

**Settings guide** — every input, what it changes, sane defaults.

## Ranking levers

- Likes and comments, early and sustained — `community` drives these.
- Description keyword coverage for how traders actually search
  (`liquidity sweep`, `kill zone`, `PDH PDL`, `MNQ`).
- The chart image on the listing is the thumbnail. It is the single highest
  leverage asset: one clean marked-up setup, no clutter, legible at
  thumbnail size. See `image`.
- Author profile completeness and consistent naming (`ai-seo` entity rules).
