# marketing-ideas

Read `product-marketing` first — most ideas die on its constraints, and
knowing them makes ideation faster rather than narrower.

## The triage filter

Every idea passes four gates before it gets effort:

1. **Permitted?** Does it survive the claim rules and platform policy?
   (`product-marketing`, `ads`)
2. **Serviceable?** If it works, can fulfillment absorb it? (`revops`)
3. **Compounding or one-shot?** A free tool compounds; a post does not.
   Prefer compounding when the backlog is long.
4. **One-operator feasible?** Ideas requiring a team are not ideas here.

Anything failing gate 1 or 2 is dead, regardless of how good it sounds.

## Current ranked backlog

Ordered by leverage per hour, given the constraints:

1. **Kill Zone Clock** (`free-tools`) — days of work, used daily, perfectly
   targeted, links passively.
2. **Publish real backtest distributions** (`content`) — we have the data and
   the engines; nobody else in the niche does this.
3. **Public delayed-signal channel** (`paywalls`, `community`) — free, and it
   is the proof asset every other channel points to.
4. **Weekly recap with losses** (`emails`, `video`) — writes itself from data
   we already produce; strongest retention lever.
5. **Alert payload guide** (`lead-magnets`) — doubles as onboarding.
6. **Prop-firm position sizer** (`free-tools`) — speaks to the real fear.
7. **Newsletter sponsorship, one insertion** (`ads`) — the only paid channel
   worth testing.
8. **Educator partnership** (`co-marketing`) — highest ceiling, slowest.
9. **Webhook payload tester** (`free-tools`) — attracts BaaS buyers.
10. **Session stats pages** (`programmatic-seo`) — only after 1–4 exist.

## Ideas explicitly rejected

Keeping the rejected list prevents re-proposing them every quarter:

- Meta/Google ads for the signal offer — ban risk (`ads`).
- Free trial of the live channel — screenshot-and-leave, expensive to fulfill
  (`paywalls`).
- Public affiliate program — attracts claim violations (`referrals`).
- Launch discount — devalues the price we intend to hold (`pricing`).
- Daily blog publishing — thin content actively hurts (`seo-audit`).
- Profit-screenshot marketing — scam marker and claim violation.

## How to generate, when the backlog runs dry

Look at what already exists and ask what it could become: an existing
backtest becomes a report; an existing question in the channel becomes an
article; an existing manual step becomes a public tool. At this scale,
repurposing beats inventing almost every time.
