# launch

Read `product-marketing` first.

## The rule that matters most

**Build the audience before the launch, not with it.** A launch broadcasts to
whoever already exists. At our scale that means the weeks of `content`,
`social`, `community` and `lead-magnets` before launch day determine the
result; launch-day tactics barely move it.

## Pre-launch (4+ weeks)

- [ ] The public Telegram channel posting every session, with outcomes.
- [ ] The free list started via a `lead-magnets` asset.
- [ ] At least one `free-tools` item live (the kill-zone clock).
- [ ] Three `/learn/` pillar articles published (`content`).
- [ ] TradingView listing complete and accurate (`aso`).
- [ ] `signup-flow` tested end to end with a real payment and a real grant.
- [ ] Fulfillment capacity confirmed (`revops`) for the expected volume.
- [ ] Risk disclosure and claim review complete (`copy-edit`).

The `copy-edit` compliance pass is a blocker, not a formality. A launch is the
worst time to discover an unsupportable claim is on the landing page.

## Launch week

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| Mon | Public channel + list announcement, plain language, price included |
| Tue | The mechanism thread on X (`social`) ending at the `/learn/` pillar |
| Wed | Walkthrough video (`video`) |
| Thu | Partner posts, if any (`co-marketing`) |
| Fri | First weekly recap including launch-week signals, losses included |

Announce with facts: what it does, who it is for, what it costs, when access
arrives. No countdowns, no "founding member" scarcity, no launch discount —
`product-marketing`'s claim rules and this audience's scam-detection both
rule them out. A launch discount also devalues the price you intend to hold
(`pricing`).

## Post-launch (first 2 weeks)

The real work. Every new subscriber goes through `onboarding` deliberately.
Watch time-to-activation daily; it predicts month-three revenue better than
signup count does. Log every question asked into `customer-research`.

## Price-change launches

Announce to existing subscribers first, before it is public, with the reason
and a grandfathering decision stated clearly. Finding out from the website
that their price changed is a churn event (`churn-prevention`).

## Definition of done

Not "we announced". Done is: new subscribers activated, fulfillment SLA held,
week-one questions logged, and the recap published. Write a short retro into
the test log (`ab-testing`).

## Cross-references

- `pricing` — the offer being launched.
- `revops` — capacity gate.
- `onboarding` — where launch value is realized.
- `social`, `emails`, `community` — the channels.
