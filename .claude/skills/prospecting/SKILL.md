# prospecting

Read `product-marketing` first.

## Principle

Outreach quality is set here, not in the writing. A researched list of 20
beats a scraped list of 2,000 — and the scraped list will burn the sending
domain (`cold-email`).

## Where the right people are

- **X/Twitter futures niche** — accounts posting MNQ/NQ charts with real
  timestamps. Sort by engagement quality, not follower count.
- **TradingView** — authors of complementary (not competing) indicators;
  their profiles show audience size honestly.
- **Prop-firm communities** — Discord owners, moderators, and the members who
  answer everyone else's questions. Those members make the best partners.
- **Newsletters** — futures and day-trading newsletters with 2k–30k
  subscribers. Above 30k the sponsorship price stops making sense at our
  margins (`ads`).
- **YouTube** — channels doing session recaps, 1k–50k subs.
- **Our own audience** — subscribers who already run communities. The warmest
  partner source we have (`referrals`, `community`).

## Qualification checklist

Before adding anyone to the list:

- [ ] Their audience is futures/index traders, not crypto or forex-general.
- [ ] Engagement looks real — comments with substance, not emoji reply spam.
- [ ] Their marketing would not violate our claim rules by association
      (`product-marketing`). Profit screenshots in their feed = skip.
- [ ] No competing signal service.
- [ ] There is a specific, nameable thing we could do together
      (`co-marketing`). "They have an audience" is not a reason.
- [ ] One concrete, recent detail recorded to open the email with
      (`cold-email` sentence one).

## The record

Per prospect: name, surface, audience size and where it was verified, the
recent detail, the proposed collaboration, and the date contacted. This feeds
the pipeline in `revops`; prospects that go nowhere are closed after 14 days.

## Volume

Cap at what can be researched properly — realistically 10–20 per week for one
operator. Resist tooling that promises 1,000 leads; at our scale it produces
nothing but spam complaints.
