# lead-magnets

Read `product-marketing` first.

## Why a list matters here

Telegram and X can suspend accounts; algorithms change; the TradingView
listing can be removed. An email list is the only audience asset we own. At
this scale it is also the cheapest path to the 20-subscriber target — a
few hundred right traders on a list, receiving the weekly recap, converts
better than any amount of cold traffic.

## Assets worth an email

Ranked by fit:

1. **The Kill Zone Cheat Sheet** — one page: London and NY windows in 12
   timezones, the levels that matter in each, and what typically sweeps first.
   Printable, pinned to a monitor, forwarded constantly.
2. **The Alert Payload Guide** — every field in the JSON (`s`,`t`,`p`,`sl`,
   `tp1`,`tp2`,`r`,`v`,`src`), what it means, and how to act on it. Doubles
   as `onboarding` material and pre-qualifies technically-minded buyers.
3. **Session Stats Report** — real distributions from `backtest_sweep.mjs`
   over NQ/ES 5m, with the range and hypothetical labeling required by
   `product-marketing`. Nobody else in the niche publishes real numbers; this
   is the differentiated asset.
4. **Prop-Firm Risk Template** — sizing sheet against drawdown rules.

## Exchange design

- **Email only.** No name, no phone, no "trading experience" dropdown. Every
  extra field costs conversion and buys nothing we use.
- Deliver instantly on the page *and* by email. Making someone wait for an
  email to get a PDF is a needless drop.
- State the follow-up honestly: "You'll also get the weekly recap. One email
  a week, unsubscribe any time." Under-promising the send rate and then
  exceeding it is how lists get marked as spam.
- No gate on the free tools themselves (`free-tools`) — ask after value.

## Placement

Inline in the matching `/learn/` article, on the relevant `/tools/` page after
use, in the public Telegram channel monthly, and via exit-intent within the
limits `popups` sets.

## Quality bar

If the asset would not stand on its own as a paid product's first chapter, it
is not worth an email address. A thin PDF trains the list to ignore us, which
is worse than not having the address.
